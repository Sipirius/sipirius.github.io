Denis Zavadski

PhD candidate · Computer Vision and Learning Lab · Heidelberg University

I work on generative image and video models. My research focuses on how to control them, how to make them cheaper to train and run, and how to use them to generate useful training data.

My PhD is supervised by Carsten Rother and Stefan Roth and funded by a scholarship from the Konrad Zuse School of Excellence in Learning and Intelligent Systems (ELIZA). Since January 2026, I have also been a visiting researcher in Stefan's group at TU Darmstadt.

From March to September 2026, I was an ML Research Intern in Vladlen Koltun's group at Apple in Zurich, where I worked on large-scale text-to-image and text-to-video diffusion and flow models, with a particular focus on video editing.

Papers

ECCV 2026

Importance-Aware Low-Rank Distillation of Diffusion Transformers

Denis Zavadski, Sebastian Heid, Damjan Kalšan, Stefan Roth, Carsten Rother

Compresses diffusion transformers such as FLUX by assigning ranks per block according to importance, truncating the least important blocks with SVD, and recovering quality through distillation. It keeps near-full quality at about 68% of the parameters.

ICLR 2026

Product-Quantised Image Representation for High-Quality Image Synthesis

Denis Zavadski, Nikita Philip Tatsch, Carsten Rother

PQGAN brings product quantisation into the VQGAN framework, giving image autoencoders higher reconstruction quality for downstream image synthesis.

3DV 2026

Online Video Depth Anything: Temporally-Consistent Depth Prediction with Low Memory Consumption

Johann-Friedrich Feiden, Tim Küchler, Denis Zavadski, Bogdan Savchynskyy, Carsten Rother

An online version of Video Depth Anything that borrows caching and frame masking from LLMs. It runs at 42 FPS on an A100 and 20 FPS on a Jetson.

arXiv

ICPR 2026 Oral

A Framework for Low-Effort Training Data Generation for Urban Semantic Segmentation

Damjan Kalšan*, Denis Zavadski*, Tim Küchler, Haebom Lee, Stefan Roth, Carsten Rother · *equal contribution

Adapts an off-the-shelf diffusion model to a target domain using only imperfect pseudo-labels, then generates aligned training images from semantic maps. It gains up to 8.0 mIoU points over prior translation methods.

ACCV 2024

PrimeDepth: Efficient Monocular Depth Estimation with a Stable Diffusion Preimage

Denis Zavadski, Damjan Kalšan, Carsten Rother

Uses a single-step Stable Diffusion "preimage" as a rich feature representation for fast, accurate monocular depth estimation.

Project pagearXivCode

ECCV 2024 Oral

ControlNet-XS: Rethinking the Control of Text-to-Image Diffusion Models as Feedback-Control Systems

Denis Zavadski, Johann-Friedrich Feiden, Carsten Rother

Treats image control as a feedback-control problem, with information flowing both ways between the control network and the base model. The control network can be as small as 1% of the base model's parameters.
